# Changelog — srd51-creatures

The creatures of the System Reference Document 5.1: the Monsters chapter and
the appendices of miscellaneous creatures and nonplayer characters, as stat
blocks (DEC-24). They live apart from srd51 so that the character sheet does
not download them: the site loads them only where creatures are shown (the
catalogue of docs/19-catalogues.md). Characters never list this package.
Like every public package, each released version is published by the site
and never changes (DEC-21).

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-25

- **Every creature of the SRD 5.1**, 322 stat blocks: armour class, hit
  points, speeds, abilities, saving throws and skills, damage and condition
  immunities, senses, languages, challenge and experience, traits, actions,
  reactions and legendary actions. Attacks, damage, saving throws and
  recharges are also given as numbers, so they can be explained and rolled.
  The vampire and the lycanthropes are one creature each, with their forms.
