# Changelog — srd51-it

The Italian translation of srd51, from the official Italian System Reference
Document 5.1 by Wizards of the Coast (CC BY 4.0). The application loads it by
itself when the interface is in Italian; characters never list it. Like every
public package, each released version is published by the site and never
changes (DEC-21).

## 0.5.0 — 2026-10-06

- **Spell and racial trait names in Title Case**, as the Italian Player's
  Handbook prints them: "Palla di Fuoco", "Alterare Se Stesso",
  "Resilienza Nanica". The owner chose one style for every name, so the
  SRD's names now match the Player's Handbook's when both appear in a list,
  and a spell's name reads as a proper name. Inside the texts, spell names
  stay in lowercase italics, as both books print them.
- "Scheda delle statistiche" everywhere, as the official books call a
  creature's stat block (it used to say "blocco di statistiche" in places).

## 0.4.0 — 2026-10-01

- **Closer to the official Italian books**, as the owner chose after
  reviewing the translation: "Dadi Vita" with capitals, spell names in
  italics in the texts, the roll tables (damage types, feather token, iron
  flask, prayer beads, reincarnate) in the order and with the ranges of the
  Italian books, subclass features and table columns with the books'
  capitals, "abilità" where the books say it.
- The barbarian's uses are "Utilizzi dell'Ira", no longer a second "Ira";
  the spell lists are "Incantesimi da Bardo", "da Chierico"…; the robe of
  useful items has the "Specchio di metallo" sold as gear; Remarkable
  Athlete adds 0,3 metri per point of Strength, as the rule means.
- The notes on what the sheet does not compute yet all begin "Non ancora
  calcolato:".
- **Follows srd51 0.9.0**: the magic items' tables are tables again (the
  wand of wonder with its own 16–20 row and 98–00, the robe of useful items
  with the ladder and the horse on two rows, reincarnate's races); the
  lists of the ability rules are lists again; darkvision lets a creature
  see in dim light as if it were bright light, word for word as the
  official Italian SRD has it.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-09-30

- **Follows srd51 0.8.0**: the unit of ki ("1 ki").

## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-26

- **Follows srd51 0.7.0**: the rules, mounts and items it added (resting,
  death saving throws, cover, movement, the chapter introductions and
  more) and every text it corrected are in Italian, from the official
  Italian SRD word for word.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-25

- **Everything in srd51 in Italian**: classes, subclasses, species,
  background, feat, spells, items, conditions, rules, tables, the ruleset's
  languages and alignments, and the ideas to start from. The texts follow the
  official Italian SRD word for word; what it does not have (the short
  reminders, the archetypes, the notes of this project) is translated with
  its terms. Distances are in metres, as in the Italian books.
