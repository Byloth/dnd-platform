# Changelog — fan-heroes

Famous characters from popular culture as the ideas to start from of the creation wizard (DEC-27): an extension,
on by default, that replaces the SRD's ideas and can be turned off from the packages page. A fan work: only the
names are borrowed; the builds, the descriptions and the reasons are the platform's own. Like every public
package, each released version is published by the site and never changes (DEC-21).

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-10

- **Twelve heroes, one per class**: Kratos, Lupin III, Shadowheart, Getafix, Geralt of Rivia, Kenshiro, Captain
  America, Legolas, Ezio Auditore, Elsa, Ghost Rider and Frieren, each with the build that makes them who they
  are in the rules and the levels where they become it.
- With the owner's Player's Handbook loaded, a hero takes the subclass and the background that suit them best
  (Shadowheart's Trickery Domain, Geralt's Eldritch Knight); with the SRD alone, the SRD's subclass and a
  background of their own.
