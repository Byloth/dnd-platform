# Phase 1 — The accessibility pass: report (M1.8b, 2026-10-06)

## What was checked

`pnpm web:a11y` (`packages/web/scripts/check-accessibility.ts`) runs on the generated site, served as GitHub Pages serves it, in Chromium (Playwright's build locally, the runner's Chrome in CI).

**27 screens:**
- characters, empty and with a stored character;
- the export dialog;
- a demo sheet, and the same sheet with the explanation drawer open;
- a stored sheet, and its delete dialog;
- the ten wizard steps, and an edit step;
- packages;
- compendium: front page, a list, an entry;
- changelog, credits, privacy, roadmap.

**Each screen is checked:**
- in English and Italian;
- in the light, dark and more-contrast themes;
- on a phone (360×740) and a computer (1280×800);
- at 320 px for reflow.

That makes **364 page loads**.

| Check | How | Result |
|---|---|---|
| axe, WCAG 2.1 A/AA and 2.2 AA, with layout (colour contrast as rendered, target size 24 px) | every screen × language × theme × viewport | **0 violations** |
| Reflow at 320 CSS px (WCAG 1.4.10) | every screen × language, light | **0 after fixes** (every screen failed before, through the navigation bar) |
| Keyboard: every visible control reached with Tab, visible when focused, with a focus indicator, nothing else focusable | every screen × language × viewport | **0 failures** |
| Escape closes the drawer and the dialogs, focus back on what opened them | export, delete, explanation | **0 failures** |
| Focus order jumping back up the page | reported for review | 6, accepted (below) |

The checker was checked too: with the focus styles removed and a heading made light grey, it reported every focused control and the contrast failure.

## Found and fixed

1. **The navigation bar overflowed at 320 px** (all screens, up to 359 px wide). Under 360 px the links' labels go to the screen reader and the icons stay, as [../09-interface-revisions.md](../09-interface-revisions.md) already planned for a bar that does not fit (`components/globals/NavigationBar.vue`).
2. **The wizard's step indicator overflowed at 320 px** (10 dots and their connectors, 332 px). Under 360 px the dots are 24 px, still the WCAG 2.2 target size, and the connectors are tighter (`components/wizard/WizardStepper.vue`).
3. **A long address in the credits widened the page** (359 px). The body now breaks long words rather than overflowing (`overflow-wrap: break-word`, `assets/scss/main.scss`).
4. **Two Italian page titles were wider than a small phone**: "Caratteristiche" and "Equipaggiamento" in Cinzel, 327 and 352 px in a 288 px column. Under 360 px `h1` is one step smaller, and headings may break a single long word with a hyphen (`hyphens: auto`, with the document's `lang`).

The quick passes were run again in English and Italian after the fixes: no failures.

## Reviewed and accepted

- **The sheet's focus order on a computer.** The sheet lays its sections out in two columns there; Tab finishes the first column ("Pin Combat to the top") before the second ("Collapse Attacks"), which is the reading order of the columns and the order of the document. On a phone, one column, there is no jump. (6 reports: the demo sheet, the stored sheet and the wizard's review, in both languages.)

## What the automation cannot do

- **The screen reader.** Names, roles and reading order are asserted in the component tests (`tests/accessibility.test.ts`, the accessible tree), but what a person hears is not. The pass is written step by step in [screen-reader-pass.md](screen-reader-pass.md), for VoiceOver, TalkBack and NVDA, about an hour; it also covers the pending passes of M1.3 and M1.4. **Waiting for a person with the device.**
- **The phone in hand**: touch, zoom with two fingers, the on-screen keyboard over the wizard's fields. Part of the same sitting.
- **Colour for colour-blind readers** beyond contrast (WCAG 1.4.1, colour not the only means): axe checks only links inside text; the rest was not checked, by simulation or by reading the components. To include in the sitting.

## Gate

`pnpm web:a11y` runs in CI after `web:generate`, next to Lighthouse (docs/phase-1/07). Locally, `CHROME_PATH` names a Chromium, for example `~/.cache/ms-playwright/chromium-*/chrome-linux64/chrome`. `--quick` checks the light theme on a phone in one language (`--language=it` for Italian), in about two minutes. The full matrix takes about half an hour.
