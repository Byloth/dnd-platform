  • When an undead fails its saving throw against your Turn Undead feature, the creature is
    instantly destroyed if its challenge rating is at or below a threshold: 1/2 at 5th level, 1 at
    8th, 2 at 11th, 3 at 14th, 4 at 17th. (Destroy Undead)

    creature regains additional hit points equal to 2 + the spell's level. (Disciple of Life)

    number of them equal to 1 + the spell's level. The chosen creatures automatically succeed on
    their saving throws against the spell, and they take no damage if they would normally take half
